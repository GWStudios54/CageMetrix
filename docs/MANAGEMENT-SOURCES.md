# MMA Scouts management-source policy

MMA Scouts separates an agency profile from fighter representation evidence.

- `profileUrls` verify that an agency exists and support MMA Scouts' original About summary.
- `urls` are explicit public fighter-roster surfaces and may create representation candidates.
- Staff, team, contact and About pages are never treated as fighter rosters.
- Missing roster evidence never means an agency has zero clients.
- Missing representation evidence never means a fighter is unmanaged.
- Conflicting current-agency claims are withheld from automatic assignment until identity/current representation can be resolved.
- Agency prestige and representation never affect Global Rating.

Every public agency page links to the official website and carries an independent-research disclaimer. MMA Scouts paraphrases factual agency information rather than reproducing marketing copy.
