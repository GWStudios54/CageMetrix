How these probabilities work

Forecast model v0.1 uses result-based Elo, with the conversion to win probability fitted on 2018–2022 bouts. Every probability is saved with its timestamp and input snapshot. A 70% favorite can still lose; probability quality is also measured using Brier score.

CMR remains a descriptive fighter rating. Its historical test has not established an overall forecasting advantage over Elo. Fighters with limited UFC history are labeled; debutants start from neutral Elo. These probabilities do not include injury reports, camp changes or betting markets.

We check UFC’s official card every two minutes during the event, and this page refreshes every 30 seconds. Result timing depends on the source publishing its decision. Original probabilities stay locked; detailed fighter ratings refresh after full statistics are verified. Statistics are attributed to their sources. Download the historical test results.
