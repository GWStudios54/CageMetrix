Lower Brier score and log loss mean better probability estimates. Accuracy counts the higher-probability fighter as the pick. A 50/50 prediction receives half credit in this benchmark; it is excluded from the live pick counter.

How Predictor 0.1 was tested

Predictor 0.1 was trained using earlier UFC fights and evaluated on 1,516 later fights that were not used to fit the final model. It reached 63.2% accuracy with a 0.228 Brier score, compared with 54.7% / 0.246 for the Elo baseline.

The live CageMetrix™ record begins at launch. Historical testing is retrospective and is not presented as previously published predictions.
