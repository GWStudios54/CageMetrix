What was tested?

The model is rebuilt before every event using earlier events only. Its opponent baselines, division populations, Elo ratings and sample penalties cannot see fights on or after the test date.

Probability conversion is fitted on 2018–2022 bouts and then frozen. Results from 2023 onward form the evaluation period. These are retrospective tests using today's corrected source data, not predictions published before those historical fights.

CMR weights are hand-set and were not optimized on this evaluation. Its overall forecasting advantage over Elo has not been established, so upcoming win probabilities use the separately labeled Elo model.

Historical comparisons require both fighters to have a prior rated UFC bout. Live forecasts involving debutants use neutral starting Elo and carry a limited-history label; their reliability has not been established by this matched-fighter benchmark.

Sample strength is an exposure index, not an accuracy probability. CMR is normalized within divisions across the available history, without explicit era or age adjustment.
